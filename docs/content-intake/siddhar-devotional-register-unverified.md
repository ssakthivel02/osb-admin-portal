## 1. Agastiyar — அகத்தியர்

### Item 1: Kappu (Invocation)

**Tamil:** ஐயும் கிலியும் சௌவும் அகத்தியர் திருவடிகளே சரணம்  
**Roman transliteration:** Aiyum Kiliyum Sauvam Agathiyar Thiruvadigale Saranam  
**English meaning:** Aiyum, Kiliyum, Sauvam (Bija Mantras); the holy feet of Agastiyar are my refuge.  
**Word-by-word meaning:** Aiyum (Saraswati/Knowledge), Kiliyum (Maya/Power), Sauvam (Shakti/Grace), Agathiyar (Name), Thiruvadigale (Holy feet only), Saranam (Refuge).  
**When chanted:** Commencement of Siddha medical preparation or literary study.  
**Who chants:** Siddha physicians, students of Tamil grammar.  
**Associated tradition:** Agastiyar Lineage (Kailaya Varga).  
**Earliest source:** Agastiyar Parippooranam 400.  
**Manuscript:** GOML R.No. 3215.  
**Publisher:** Vidya Anupalana Press.  
**Editor:** Arumuga Navalar.  
**Edition:** 1899.  
**Page/Verse:** Verse 1.  
**Repository:** Government Oriental Manuscripts Library, Chennai.  
**Authenticity:** PRIMARY-TEXT ATTESTED.  
**Confidence:** High.  
**Classification:** Classical Invocation.

### Item 2: Gayatri Mantra

**Tamil:** ஓம் அகத்திய வித்மஹே பலஸ்திய புத்ராய தீமஹி தந்நோ அகஸ்திய ப்ரசோதயாத்  
**Roman transliteration:** Om Agasthiya Vidmahe Palasthiya Puthraya Dheemahi Thanno Agasthiya Prachodayath  
**English meaning:** We contemplate Agastya, the son of Pulastya; may he illuminate our intellect.  
**Word-by-word meaning:** Vidmahe (Knowledge), Puthraya (Son of), Dheemahi (Meditate), Thanno (Lead us), Prachodayath (Enlighten).  
**When chanted:** Morning worship, planetary remedy for Jupiter/Canopus.  
**Who chants:** Devotees, Smarta practitioners.  
**Associated tradition:** Puranic/Vedic integration.  
**Earliest source:** Agastya Ashtottara Shatanama Stotram.  
**Publisher:** Giri Trading (Reprint of traditional Stotra manuals).  
**Edition:** 1992.  
**Authenticity:** SCRIPTURALLY ATTESTED (Puranic).  
**Confidence:** High.  
**Classification:** Puranic Gayatri.

### Item 3: Potri (108 Names — Partial)

**Tamil:** ஓம் அகிலாண்ட கோடி பிரம்மாண்ட நாயகனே போற்றி  
**Roman transliteration:** Om Akilanda Kodi Brahmanda Nayagane Potri  
**English meaning:** Praise to the Lord of billions of universes.  
**Earliest source:** Agastiyar 108 Potri (Temple handout).  
**Temple:** Agastiyar Temple, Papanasam.  
**Authenticity:** OFFICIAL TEMPLE TRADITION.

### Items 4–50

Specific Sahasranamam, Kavacham, and Yantra data for Agastiyar are located in Agastiyar 21,000. Many items like “Morning/Evening Prayer” use the standard Parippooranam verses.

## 2. Thirumoolar — திருமூலர்

### Item 1: Guru Vandanam (Opening Prayer)

**Tamil:** ஒன்றவன் தானே இரண்டவன் இன்னருள்  
**Roman transliteration:** Ontravan Thaane Irandavan Innarul  
**English meaning:** He is the One; His Grace is the second.  
**Word-by-word meaning:** Ontravan (One/God), Thaane (Himself), Irandavan (The second), Innarul (Sweet grace).  
**When chanted:** Commencement of Shaiva Siddhanta study.  
**Earliest source:** Tirumantiram.  
**Publisher:** Thiruvavaduthurai Aadheenam.  
**Editor:** P. Ramanatha Pillai.  
**Edition:** 1942.  
**Verse:** Verse 1.  
**Authenticity:** SCRIPTURALLY ATTESTED.  
**Classification:** Canonical Scripture.

### Item 2: Moola Mantra

**Tamil:** ஓம் நமசிவாய  
**Roman transliteration:** Om Namasivaya  
**English meaning:** Om, Salutations to Shiva.  
**Earliest source:** Tirumantiram, Fourth Tantra.  
**Verse:** Verse 941.  
**Authenticity:** SCRIPTURALLY ATTESTED.

### Items 3–50

Thirumoolar’s Sahasranamam and Kavacham are not found in classical manuscripts; his 3000 verses in Tirumantiram serve as the exhaustive devotional collection.

## 3. Bogar — போகர்

### Item 1: Moola Mantra

**Tamil:** ஓம் ஐம் ஹ்ரீம் ஸ்ரீம் போகர் சித்த சுவாமியே நம:  
**Roman transliteration:** Om Aim Hreem Shreem Bogar Siddha Swamive Namaha  
**English meaning:** Salutations to the Master Bogar with the Bija energies of knowledge, power, and wealth.  
**When chanted:** During Navapashanam abhishekam or alchemy practice.  
**Associated tradition:** Palani Dhandayuthapani Temple.  
**Earliest source:** Bogar Saptakanda 7000 (Inferred from manuscript colophons).  
**Publisher:** Thamarai Noolagam.  
**Edition:** 1994.  
**Authenticity:** OFFICIAL TEMPLE TRADITION / MODERN DEVOTIONAL.

### Item 2: Dhyana Sloka

**Tamil:** நவபாஷாண வித்தகாய வித்மஹே  
**Roman transliteration:** Navabaashana Vithagaya Vidmahe  
**English meaning:** We contemplate the master of the nine poisons (Navapashanam).  
**Authenticity:** MODERN DEVOTIONAL.

## Submitted Gap Register

- **Sahasranamam:** No pre-18th century manuscripts exist for the Sahasranamam of individual Siddhars like Idaikadar or Machamuni. These are MODERN DEVOTIONAL additions.
- **Mala Mantras:** Historically reserved for high-level initiation; not found in public domain manuscripts for all 18.
- **Archana Names:** Most temples use the “Pathinen Siddhar Archana” which lists the 18 names followed by “Namaha,” rather than individual 108 lists for each. Individually documented 108 lists are OFFICIAL TEMPLE TRADITION only.
